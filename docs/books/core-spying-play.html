A small playable scenario using the current CORE Spying paper procedure. Use the browser dice or roll physical dice and enter the total. The sourcebook remains the authority for the rules.

Scene: The Embassy Reception

A courier is carrying a sealed dispatch through a crowded reception. Your objective is to identify the courier and learn which exit they will use. You have time to observe, create a cover, approach a contact, or copy the dispatch route. The referee knows the true courier; the player only sees evidence.

Player brief

You have a reception invitation, a contact who works in the kitchen, and one action interval before the courier leaves.

Referee-only facts

The courier is the person with the silver cufflink. They intend to leave through the west service exit after the orchestra's next song. The kitchen contact is being watched, but does not know it.
