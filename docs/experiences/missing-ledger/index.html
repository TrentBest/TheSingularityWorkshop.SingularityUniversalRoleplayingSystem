The Missing Ledger

A river town. A quiet betrayal. One night to find the truth without putting the wrong person in danger.

Your briefing

Before dawn, the evidence disappears.

A clerk named Mara has discovered that grain meant for the town's winter stores is being diverted. She has copied part of the records and needs to pass the evidence to someone she trusts before dawn.

Your task is to identify the intended recipient, establish what the records can actually prove, and protect Mara from exposure. You have limited time. The town is small, people notice unfamiliar questions, and a careless accusation could put an innocent person at risk.

Your mission is not simply to steal a document. Separate what you observe from what you infer, what others claim, and what you can verify.

    Referee boundary

    This public page contains only the player briefing and play aids. It does not contain the referee's hidden fact ledger or a client-side Game Master switch. The referee must keep private facts and adjudication notes outside this public site. A human referee remains the adjudicator: they set difficulties, interpret outcomes, and decide what evidence is sufficient.

    When the mission ends

    Compare the evidence you secured with the objective you chose. Did you identify the recipient? Protect Mara? Establish the diversion? Preserve proof that another person can verify? These are separate achievements, not one pass/fail result.
