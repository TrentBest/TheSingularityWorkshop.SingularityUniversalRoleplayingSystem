The grounds

The map falls away. The road narrows, the trees thin, and the property comes into view: an estate whose restraint is more unsettling than ostentation. Warm windows, pale stone, immaculate grounds—and security that does not belong to a country house.

The property is too well kept to be abandoned, too quiet to be welcoming. Two armed guards stand at the entrance. Between them, beneath the portico, an attendant waits with a ledger.

At the entrance

The attendant looks up from the ledger. The guards do not move. He studies your papers, then your face.
