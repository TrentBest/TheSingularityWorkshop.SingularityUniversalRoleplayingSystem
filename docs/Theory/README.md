## Editorial position

Roleplaying did not begin with one videogame, one rulebook, or one technology. Its history is a convergence: campaign wargaming, individual-character play, referee-mediated improvisation, shared imagined worlds, procedural rules, and later interactive computing.

SURPS studies those traditions to identify reusable ideas. It does not claim that every title called an RPG uses the same mechanics, nor that digital games are simply tabletop games with a screen. Each medium changes the relationship between rules, participants, time, space, authorship, and consequence.

The theory pages are historical interpretation and design analysis. They are not a claim that the SURPS runtime already implements every described capability.
