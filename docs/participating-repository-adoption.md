"Integrated" does not mean fully admitted or runtime-conformant. The registry status remains authoritative until SURPS independently verifies the repository's manifest, catalogue, ownership, and implementation evidence.

## Shared acceptance criteria

Every active participant and integrated domain must:

- link the two canonical SURPS standards;
- identify its authoritative domain behavior and provider;
- present a big-picture-first living guide;
- provide a concise player path and a deep Game Master/Workshop path;
- distinguish tabletop and digital adapter maturity;
- show major concepts with meaningful living pictures and mechanics with worked or interactive examples;
- offer a playable Pages slice when Pages is enabled;
- preserve provenance and explicitly label prototypes;
- derive tabletop procedures from behavior through workshop calibration when the underlying evaluator exists;
- cache immutable compiled behavior, while evaluating current mutable world state at runtime.

## Adoption is evidence-based

This tracker records the work requested, not completed conformance. An issue can close only when the repository has a reviewable implementation and evidence. A landing page, a manifest entry, or a diagram by itself is not sufficient proof of a playable adapter or a validated behavior evaluator.

Future agents should read this tracker, the two canonical standards, and the linked repository issue before changing a participating book or Pages experience.
